A01/08 [ RUGBY UNLEASHED ] Brand Potential Benelux · 2024–25 · Executive Creative Director Brand creationPlatforms that last

Gallagher PREM

Unpolished beauty.

[ Tension ]

Double weekly reach from 1.9m to 3.8m: keep loyal fans, win casual sports fans and honour the heritage.

[ Idea ]

Stop polishing the sport. A name fans actually say, a wordmark that leans in, the sponsor built into the mark.

[ Result ]

687m social views (+90%) and stadiums at 82% capacity, a league record. Every fixture live on TNT Sports.

[ Role ]

I set the direction and led the team from strategy through identity, motion and rollout.

A02/08 [ ONE NIGHT FOR THE WHOLE GAME ] Brand Potential Benelux · 2025 · Identity and creative assets Brand creation

The Rugby Awards

One night for the whole game.

[ Tension ]

Four bodies (Premiership Rugby, RPA, RFU and PWR) and a brand-new awards night with no visual history.

[ Idea ]

A celebratory identity: a palette taken from the season's moments, graphics that move like the ball.

[ Result ]

A modular system across digital, print and the live event for the first Rugby Awards.

A03/08 [ PROXIMITY IS POWER ] Brand Potential Benelux · 2024–25 · Executive Creative Director · team of 10 Brand creationExperience and retail

Rotterdam The Hague Airport

Proximity is power.

[ Tension ]

A regional airport competing on a scale it could never win. Its real strength, closeness, was undervalued.

[ Idea ]

Dichtbij: civic pride for Rotterdam and South Holland, and two minutes from door to plane.

[ Craft ]

One continuous line draws the control tower and a tail fin. The system runs from fire trucks to cafes and wayfinding.

[ Role ]

I led 10 people from strategy to a full-airport rollout, aligning government, airlines and residents.

A04/08 [ SOCIAL IMPACT ] HarrimanSteel · 2020–22 · Creative Director · team of about 20 Platforms that last

Uber

Reveal the action.

[ Tension ]

Uber's safety, social and community work was scattered and understated. Self-praise in a pandemic was the risk.

[ Idea ]

Audit everything, unite it in one ecosystem, and tell it through earners and partners, with proof.

[ Result ]

An impact home on uber.com, Everyday Giants, Keys to the City rewards and Noor photojournalism in 11 cities.

[ Role ]

I led about 20 people across strategy, creative, production and external partners.

SUB-PROJECTEveryday Giants. Hero the person, not the platform. A 60-second TVC, five hand-painted murals in four US cities, social and OOH, then UK, France and global phases.
A05/08 [ POWER ON, GAME ON ] Studio Bonjour Design · Founder, ECD and executive producer Platforms that lastBrand creation

Canyon

Sell the attitude, not the motor.

[ Tension ]

E-MTBs were sold as bikes with a motor, on range and specs. The category had no voice.

[ Idea ]

Power On, Game On: one attitude-led platform for the whole :ON family, told through riders and play.

[ Result ]

Every model sold out in its launch window, the first in 24 hours. Four more launches followed.

[ Role ]

Strategy, creative direction and production, plus The Crossing series.

A07/08 [ HAND THEM THE MIC ] HarrimanSteel · Creative Director · Jordan Brand EMEA Platforms that last

Jordan Wings

Launch an American brand's education programme in Paris without looking like a corporate saviour.
